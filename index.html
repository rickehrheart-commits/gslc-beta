Android Users

Download GSLC for Android

Version 1.0.16. This is a test version and is not on the Google Play Store.

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download GSLC for Android.
  2. If your browser asks permission to install an app, allow it for this download only. After the app is installed, you can turn that permission back off in your browser’s app settings.
  3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning for this file, choose Install anyway for this app only. Do not turn off Play Protect for your whole phone.
  4. Open the downloaded file and follow the prompts.

If Good Shepherd is already installed, choose Update. Do not uninstall the current app first.
